Wave 13 — spatial volumes and full orbit exploration

User request: replace photo sheets and visibly triangulated objects with three-dimensional objects in every scene, with dense realistic details and full orbit viewing.
Accepted preference: structures appropriate to object type, including naturally flattened galaxies; desktop quality priority with mobile reductions.

Implementation
- Shared closed-boundary XYZ density renderer integrates light through real depth.
- Orion/Pleiades gas, molecular dust and spatial stars replace photo sheets.
- M31 stellar disk, thick component, bulge and dust; LMC asymmetric stellar bar and nurseries replace flattened photo surfaces.
- Vega has a finite dust volume and spatial grains.
- Black hole gas disk has nonzero height. Stellar prominence arcs have closed tube cross-sections.
- Existing Earth, stellar spheres, icy bodies, navigation, type size, panels and audio controls reused.
- Photo references supply colors and morphology within simulated density fields; credits and bilingual model descriptions updated.
- Mobile volumes use half the sampling count and fewer spatial stars. Nebula raster budget bounds per-frame workload without reducing desktop volume layers.

Limits
- Unobserved depths/back sides are models, not measured reconstructions. Cluster star positions are illustrative, not a precise catalogue.
- Black-hole lens-like glow is artistic; this is not full general-relativistic ray tracing.
- Distant interstellar photographic panorama remains on a spherical sky boundary with spatial nearby stars; it is not a reconstruction of every distant star.
- Performance observations are frame intervals on this computer. Real mobile hardware and every GPU were not tested. Heavy scenes compile on first entry and may pause briefly.
