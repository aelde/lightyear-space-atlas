Verified 2026-10-13 in the current checkout before publication.

User instruction: environmental audio should be the main sound, with Earth
water/birds/nature and fiery-star effects instead of a shared musical drone.
Interpretation: use a selected terrestrial habitat and designed object-specific
textures, clearly label actual field recordings versus artistic sound design.
No claim of physical microphone recordings from distant stars or galaxies.

Implementation
- Removed six musical/harmonic drones and the previous travel whoosh.
- Earth mixes two verified CC0 recordings, 29.3 seconds with loop overlap.
- Other scenes use 8 distinct nonmusical procedural texture families plus a
  nature-like fallback. Scene mappings cover Earth and all12 landmarks.
- Existing sound button/slider/mixer are reused, with scene title and expandable
  explanation/credits. Default remains silent until a real user gesture.
- Real post-master RMS amplitude drives the meter. It is not a spectrum,
  temperature, terrain measurement, star energy or physical loudness metric.
- Environment audio is independent of the cinematic VFX clock; no physical
  event synchronization or scientific data sonification is claimed.

Limits
