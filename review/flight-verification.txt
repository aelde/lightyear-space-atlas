LIGHTYEAR — free-space 3D journey, wave3
User direction: free floating through space, without a ship or cockpit frame.
Changes: full-screen WebGL scene, true sphere Earth/NASA maps/clouds, emissive stars, depth-bearing nebula gas, galaxy disk and particles. Smooth camera journeys with drag/orbit, zoom, pause, destination selector, tour and source-backed detail dialogs.
Reused: original radius.js 0–10 light-year component, data/source links, destination rail/filter and landmark detail dialog from wave2.
