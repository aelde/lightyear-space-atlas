LIGHTYEAR — dense visual detail across all scenes
2026-10-13. User scope: improve ALL scenes to feel as detailed as Earth; nebula
style should resemble astronomical photographs, with dense fine structure.

Current implementation was inspected and screenshots captured before editing.
Observed: Orion was faint, smooth, additive smoke with four round bright dots;
the red photosphere was freckled noise; galaxies lacked fine dark dust structure.
Custom output shaders lacked the tone-mapping transform used by Earth's surface.

Changes
- Earth surface/cloud maps, geometry, rotation and cloud-weather code retained.
  Earth atmospheric shells keep the previous un-tonemapped output appearance.
- New reusable stellar surfaces: evolving cellular convection, fine filaments,
  dark magnetic regions, derivative relief; blue-white photospheres with softer
  contrast; brown-dwarf advected copper cloud decks and vortices.
- Real spherical corona shells, weather strands and plasma particles; finer
  partially emissive loop strands; broad smooth Vega disk plus9200dust particles.
- Oort: expanded explanatory irregular icy body with fine ridge/grain shading,
  distantSun and sparse surrounding objects, not a wall of icy debris.
- Interstellar: licensed real whole-sky photo projected onto a dome, with distant
  star points. This is anEarth-view panorama used illustratively, not measured
  sky coordinates from a location2–3lightyears away.
- Orion and Pleiades:4K observational textures on curved layered gas sheets;
  dark dust attenuates background; photograph-colored3D points remain at side
  angles. Relief, layering and slow advection are illustrative, not recovered3D.
- Galaxies: observational textures blended into inclined disk/stellar populations,
  diffuse bulge, fine dust lanes and asymmetricalLMC star-forming structures.
- Black hole: differential orbiting gas filaments, disk thickness and illustrative
  far-side light arcs; no claim of exactGR orEHT reconstruction.
- Reused inspector expanded from7 to13scenes, with39feature points total.
  Credits/source images/filter-colour interpretation and licences are visible.
- Explicit image-loading feedback; uploaded textures are not marked ready before
  actual image data arrives. Sources are locally hosted, CC BY4.0 attributed.
