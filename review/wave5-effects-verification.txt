LIGHTYEAR wave5 — animated phenomena, optional audio and visual inspection
User clarification: visible cloud flow, slow rotation, rings/debris and solar-style plasma eruptions.

Implementation:
- Original opt-in Web Audio stereo ambient layers and short transition sweeps. No AudioContext is created on initial load. Master level fades; mute eventually suspends the context. Volume 0–100%, live RMS meter, visibility lifecycle handling.
- Animated magnetic plasma ribbons with changing height, brightness and halo, outgoing plasma particles, photospheric granulation/advection; applied to Proxima and Barnard concepts.
- Separate Earth surface/cloud rotation and latitude-dependent cloud texture advection; brown dwarf atmospheric flow.
- Broad faint Vega debris disk and differently paced particle orbits; inner debris moves faster than outer. No rigid ring object is claimed.
- Orion volumetric density/wisps flow locally; rigid rotation of Orion and whole galaxies removed. Sagittarius hot-gas texture and particles circulate with radial speed variation.
- Optional inspector: 7 source-backed entries, 21 annotated feature types, metrics and sources. Effect tempo 0.5/1/2/4x. It is an explanatory scene, not a measured real-time data feed or GR simulation.
- Reused existing radius, destination rail/filter and detail dialogs. Direct scene query supported, e.g. ?scene=centauri.
